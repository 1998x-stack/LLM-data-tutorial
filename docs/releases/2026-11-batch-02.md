## Editorial safeguards

- URL path/query are not lowercased indiscriminately; only explicit tracking parameters may be removed.
- Bloom Filter "maybe" is never treated as exact membership in the recommended architecture.
- LSH probability is presented as candidate-generation probability, not final dedup accuracy.
- Transitive duplicate clusters are distinguished from pairwise similarity.
- FineWeb's global/per-crawl findings are scoped to its own snapshot and ablation setup.
- Perplexity is explicitly treated as target-domain fit, not universal quality.
